Our Roots & Mission

About Natomas Sport Association

Building high-caliber youth athletic pathways that keep Sacramento talent thriving right here in Natomas.

Why We Were Founded

Keeping Elite Athletic Pathways Close To Home

For years, Natomas families seeking competitive travel sports had to spend hours commuting to Roseville, Rocklin, or Elk Grove. The Natomas Sport Association was established to give local families a premier competitive home right here in North Natomas.

We utilize Natomas’s world-class public parks and regional fields to offer competitive travel baseball teams and disciplined player development. We combine high-level coaching with intense energy, high character standards, and adult-modeled respect.

Our Mission

To develop physically resilient, mentally tough, and coachable youth athletes through competitive sport.

Our Community

Proudly serving North Natomas, South Natomas, Westlake, Valley Oak, and surrounding Sacramento neighborhoods.

The Association Standards

1
Effort & Focus:

Control what you can control: your energy, preparation, hustle, and body language.

2
Respect & Integrity:

Honor your teammates, opponents, coaches, and game officials with class.

3
Continuous Growth:

Strikeouts and errors are feedback. We embrace challenges to get better every practice.

4
Community Pride:

We represent the Natomas community with pride on every tournament diamond.
