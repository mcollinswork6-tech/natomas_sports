Coaching Philosophy

Teaching The Fundamentals Of Winning Baseball

Travel baseball shouldn't just be about collecting weekend plastic trophies. At Natomas Sports Association, our priority is preparing athletes to excel as they advance towards high school and collegiate ball.

01

Arm Care & Science-Backed Pitch Counts

Strict adherence to youth pitching rest thresholds and throwing regimens to safeguard young arms.

02

Situational IQ & Decision Making

Teaching players to anticipate plays before the pitch, understand pitch counts, and execute aggressive baserunning.

03

Small Roster Philosophy

We cap our rosters at 11-12 players per team so every athlete earns meaningful defensive innings and plate appearances.

Competitive Age Divisions

Teams are organized by player birth year / USSSA age classification.

9U / 10U Developmental

Junior Minors Division

Transition to kid-pitch with 46/60 field dimensions. Focus on throwing mechanics, tracking fly balls, plate discipline, and catching technique.

Base Paths: 60 ft | Mound: 46 ft

Commitment: 2x practices/wk + 1-2 weekend tourneys/mo

11U / 12U Majors

Intermediate Majors

Expanded 50/70 dimensions introducing open bases, lead-offs, balks, and pickoffs. Players refine secondary pitches (changeups) and situational defense.

Base Paths: 70 ft | Mound: 50 ft

Commitment: 2x practices/wk + 2 weekend tourneys/mo

13U / 14U High School Prep

Full Diamond Prep

Full regulation 60/90 diamond play preparing players for high school varsity rosters. Strength, speed, and advanced scouting.

Base Paths: 90 ft | Mound: 60 ft 6 in

Commitment: 2-3x practices/wk + 2 tourneys/mo
