Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the time commitment for Bad Hops 11U travel baseball?

Our 11U team typically practices twice per week on weekday evenings in Natomas, with 1 to 2 weekend tournaments per month. During tournament weekends, teams play 3 to 5 games across Saturday and Sunday. We structure our calendar to prevent player burnout.

Is there any fee to attend player evaluations?

No. Tryouts and evaluations are 100% free of charge. We simply ask all players to pre-register online through our Tryouts page so our coaching staff can plan stations and age groups.

Can my child play Little League alongside Bad Hops travel baseball?

Yes, we actively encourage multi-sport balance and local league participation. Bad Hops 11U schedules tournaments and practices with family schedules in mind to support well-rounded athletic development.
