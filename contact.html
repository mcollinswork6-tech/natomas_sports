Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the time commitment for travel baseball?

NSA teams typically practice twice per week on weekday evenings in Natomas, with 1 to 2 weekend tournaments per month. During tournament weekends, teams play 3 to 5 games across Saturday and Sunday. We structure our calendar to prevent player burnout.

Is there any fee to attend a tryout?

No. Tryouts and evaluations are 100% free of charge. We simply ask all players to pre-register online through our Tryouts page so our coaching staff can plan stations and age groups.

Can my child play Little League alongside NSA?

Yes, many of our 9U and 10U athletes also participate in local Little League seasons. As players advance to 12U and 14U, travel tournament commitments often take primary focus, but we actively encourage multi-sport balance.

When will basketball and other sports begin?

Our multi-sport rollout starts with winter/fall youth basketball clinics and 3v3 leagues, followed by spring soccer and seasonal speed/agility sessions. You can submit your interest on our Sports & Programs page.
