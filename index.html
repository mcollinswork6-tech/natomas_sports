Section 02 • Core Principles

Our 6 Organization Values

Our values are not abstract ideas. They guide everyday decisions for every team, coach, athlete, board member, and volunteer within the Natomas Sport Association.

Value 01

Youth First

Protect player welfare, safety, enjoyment, and age-appropriate development in all athletic activities, training programs, and competition formats.

Player welfare always takes precedence over winning.
Value 02

Development

Value continuous learning, skill progression, mental resilience, and thoughtful competition where young athletes learn to overcome challenges.

Progressive skill development and physical longevity.
Value 03

Integrity

Act honestly, follow rules, manage resources responsibly, and make every decision to advance the organization’s charitable and developmental purpose.

Ethical leadership and transparent stewardship.
Value 04

Respect

Treat players, families, game officials, tournament opponents, volunteers, and coaches with dignity at all times—win or lose.

Sportsmanship and composure under pressure.
Value 05

Accountability

Set clear expectations across teams, accept feedback constructively, communicate transparently about dues and schedules, and address concerns fairly.

Defined standards and documented escalation paths.
Value 06

Community

Create a welcoming sense of belonging for families, connect with local partners, and offer meaningful opportunities for community members to contribute.

Rooted in Natomas & Greater Sacramento families.
Section 03 • Culture Standards

The Association Culture Standard

"Culture is not a slogan. It is what leaders, coaches, and families consistently do, especially when there is disagreement, pressure to win, or an unexpected problem."

The Adult-First Commitment

Shared Standards
01

Board & Leaders

Model ethical decisions, follow bylaws and approved policies, manage conflicts of interest, oversee finances, and respond to concerns without favoritism.

02

Coaches

Teach and challenge without humiliation; communicate expectations clearly; model sportsmanship and composure; prioritize player welfare and development.

03

Parents & Guardians

Support all athletes, respect officials, raise concerns through established channels, and avoid gossip, public confrontations, and involving children in adult disputes.

04

Volunteers

Follow assigned roles, safety procedures, confidentiality expectations, and the organization’s conduct standards to ensure fair and organized operations.

05

Athletes

Be coachable, compete fairly, support teammates through victories and setbacks, and tell a trusted adult when something feels unsafe, inappropriate, or wrong.

Non-Negotiable Culture Commitments

Zero Tolerance for Misconduct & Retaliation

  • • No bullying, hazing, harassment, retaliation, or public humiliation of any player, parent, coach, or official.
  • • No recruiting children into adult conflicts or using youth athletes to relay grievances.
  • • No disparaging other organizations as a recruiting strategy.
  • • Clear, respectful communication about expectations, team fees, schedules, and decisions.
  • • Player safety allegations require prompt handling through documented reporting without delay.
Our Definition of Success

More Than Just the Scoreboard

"Competitive results matter, but Natomas Sport Association also measures success through player growth, retention, sportsmanship, positive family experiences, and the trust it earns through consistent behavior."

Constructive Conflict Resolution:

Listen first. Identify specific concerns. Move sensitive conversations to private settings. Avoid heated postgame confrontations. Follow a documented escalation path.

Bad Hops 11U: Built Bolder for Development & Confidence

Bad Hops is our flagship independent 11U travel baseball program—bringing intensity to the field, humility to the game, and an uncompromising commitment to helping every player improve. We keep our 11U roster tight to guarantee every athlete receives quality repetitions, situational reps, and meaningful tournament play.

Sound Skill Instruction:

Consistent batting cage work, defensive footwork, cutoffs, rotational mechanics, and arm-saving pitch count tracking.

Transparent Roster Management:

Clear expectations, playing time earned through practice and effort, and straightforward tournament communications.

Supportive Dugout Culture:

High energy, respectful sportsmanship, relentless encouragement, and team camaraderie that builds lasting friendships.
