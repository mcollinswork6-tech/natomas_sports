✓ 100% Free Player Evaluations
⚾ What Will Be Assessed
  • • Baserunning: Leads, jumps, first-to-third speed & base running IQ.
  • • Arm Mechanics & Velocity: Infield & outfield throwing mechanics, accuracy & arm speed.
  • • Defensive Fielding: Position-specific glove work, ground balls, fly balls & footwork.
  • • Batting & Exit Velocity: Live cage evaluation, swing path, contact quality & plate discipline.
  • • Hustle & Attitude: Coachability, on-field communication, effort, and team energy.
Scored on mechanics, athleticism & coachability.
🧢 Uniform & Required Gear
  • • Assessment Uniform: Baseball pants (white or grey preferred), baseball cap, athletic jersey or workout shirt, belt & socks.
  • • Footwear: Molded rubber cleats or turf shoes.
    ⚠️ STRICTLY NO METAL CLEATS (11U safety rule).
  • • Protective Cup: Mandatory for all catchers and infielders.
  • • Required Equipment: Fielding glove, USSSA approved bat (1.15 BPF / USA), batting helmet, and water bottle.
  • • Catchers: Bring full catcher's gear if evaluating behind the plate.
Label all bats, gloves & helmets with player name.
